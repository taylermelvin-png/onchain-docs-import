# Rank System

Your rank is your standing in the Colosseum — the seasonal climb from Recruit to Immortal. Rank determines one thing, and it's a big one: your **[cashback rate](cashback.md)** on every trading fee you pay.

## The Six Ranks

Six clean ranks, no sub-tiers. The current ladder:

| Rank | Rank points to reach | Cashback rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruit | 0 | 25% |
| Warrior | 10,000 | 30% |
| Gladiator | 20,000 | 35% |
| Champion | 50,000 | 40% |
| Warlord | 350,000 | 45% |
| Immortal | 1,000,000 | 50% |

Rank up and the higher cashback rate applies to every trade from that moment on.

***

## Three counters — don't mix them up

| Counter | What it is | Resets? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rank points** | Your seasonal climb — earned from trading and quests, they set your rank | Yes — at each season open, everyone drops to Recruit together |
| **Points balance** | Your lifetime points total | Never |
| **Season leaderboard** | Points earned this season, ranked against other traders for the prize pool | Each season |

Your **lifetime points balance is never reset and never spent** — it's your permanent record. Rank points are the seasonal race; the leaderboard is the prize race. The same trading feeds all three.

***

## Earning Rank Points

Every product earns rank points per dollar of volume — at different rates, on purpose:

| Activity | Rank points per $1 |
| --- | --- |
| **Trenches** trades | 0.75 — the highest rate on the terminal |
| **Swaps** | 0.50 |
| **Predictions** trades | 0.50 |
| **Spot sells** | 0.35 |
| **Perps** volume (position held 60s+) | 0.05 |
| Perps closed under 60 seconds | 0.025 |
| Stable-to-stable swaps | 0.25 — deliberately the lowest-effort, lowest-earn lane |

Two perps wrinkles: hold a position for at least a minute and you earn the full rate, and a position that closes on a major move earns a **1.5× rate — wins and losses alike**. Spot **buys** earn nothing — there's no fee on a spot buy, so the earn sits on the sell.

**Quests** pay rank points on top of trading — daily and weekly quests are the steadiest way to accelerate a climb. Occasional **Bonus Windows**, faction boosts, and season events can multiply point earnings, but they're the exception now — the base rates are where the climb lives.

***

## The Season Reset

Rank points reset to zero when a new season opens — **everyone** returns to Recruit together, whales and OGs included. Your cashback rate resets with it and climbs back as you rank up (grandfathered OG traders reset to the OG Recruit rate — see [Cashback](cashback.md)).

The reset costs your position, not your progress: your lifetime points balance and your claimable cashback are untouched, and the ladder is the same distance for everyone.
