# Trenches Overview

Trenches is onchain.cc's token-launch product — a live scanner and trading surface for new Solana tokens, from the first block of a launch through graduation and beyond. If your game is catching runners early, this is where you live.

***

## What Trenches Covers

Trenches tracks launches across **three launchpads**:

| Launchpad | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **PumpFun** | The highest-volume Solana memecoin launchpad. Tokens graduate to PumpSwap. |
| **LaunchLab** | Raydium's launchpad. Tokens graduate to Raydium pools. |
| **Meteora** | Meteora's dynamic bonding curve launches. Tokens graduate to Meteora pools. |

Every token shows a launchpad badge on its logo, and you can filter any view to the launchpads you care about. Trading works the same way across all three — Trenches handles each venue's mechanics under the hood, both before and after graduation.

{% hint style="info" %}
Trenches is currently **Solana-only** — that's where the launchpad market lives.
{% endhint %}

***

## The Tabs

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Live** | The real-time scanner: three columns — **New**, **Bonding**, **Graduated** — streaming launches as they happen. |
| **Explorer** | The filtered browser: Pumping / All / New / Gainers / Watchlist views with deep filters. |
| **Positions** | Your Trenches bags — value, open positions, unrealized PnL, quick sell. |
| **Tracker** | Wallet tracking: follow up to 50 wallets and get live alerts on their moves. |
| **Alerts** | The live feed of signals — graduations, whale buys, dev exits, volume spikes, and your tracked wallets' activity. On desktop this lives in the right rail; on mobile it's its own tab. |

## The Typical Flow

1. **Spot it** — a token appears in the Live scanner or Explorer, or one of your tracked wallets buys in.
2. **Vet it** — check the holder-analysis chips (dev / sniper / bundler / insider), bonding progress, volume, and socials. See [Finding Tokens](discovery.md).
3. **Snipe it** — one tap with your saved preset: amount, slippage, and priority settings pre-configured. See [Trading on Trenches](trading.md).
4. **Manage it** — watch the position in the Positions tab, track the chart, and sell in one tap when it's time.

***

## Fees

Trenches trades pay the same rank-based platform fee as [Swap](../swap/fees.md) — **0.15% at the base rank, falling to 0.10%** as your [Colosseum rank](../colosseum/ranks.md) climbs. Launchpads charge their own protocol fees on bonding-curve trades, which are part of the quoted price. Standard Solana gas and priority fees apply.

***

{% hint style="warning" %}
Launchpad tokens are the highest-risk corner of crypto. Most launches never graduate, and many lose most of their value within hours. Trade only with capital you are willing to lose entirely.
{% endhint %}
