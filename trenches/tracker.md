# Wallet Tracker

The best traders don't just watch tokens — they watch wallets. The **Tracker** tab lets you follow up to **50 Solana wallets**, get live alerts when they move, and see their trades plotted on your charts.

***

## Wallets

Add any Solana wallet by address, give it a nickname, and organise wallets into colour-coded **groups**. For every tracked wallet you can see:

* **PnL** — how the wallet has actually performed
* **Alerts** — toggle live notifications per wallet or per group
* **Chart bubbles** — plot the wallet's buys and sells directly on token charts

### Import your list

Bring your existing tracking list with you:

* **Paste from other terminals** — imports from Trojan, Axiom, and GMGN lists are supported, along with most common formats.
* **Import Top Traders** — one click seeds a curated group of high-signal trader wallets to get you started.

Your tracker syncs with your account, so it follows you across devices.

## Alerts

The Alerts view streams your tracked wallets' activity live:

| Alert type | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy** | A tracked wallet buys a token |
| **Sell** | A tracked wallet sells |
| **Token Launch** | A tracked wallet deploys a new token |

Each alert shows the wallet, token, size, and price — click through to the token to act on it. Filter the feed by alert type and trade size. Alerts also flow into the Trenches signal strip and the alerts rail, so they're visible wherever you're working — as a pinned side rail, a floating pop-out, or the Alerts Feed [Dashboard widget](../portfolio/dashboard.md).

***

## Monitor

The Monitor view flips the lens from wallets to tokens: it groups activity by token, showing **which of your tracked wallets bought or sold what** — with their combined holdings, entries, and exits per token. When three wallets you respect all show up in the same launch, Monitor is where you see it first.

***

## Wallet Analyzer

Click any wallet for the full workup:

* **Positions and activity** — current bags and recent trades
* **Performance** — PnL over 1/7/30 days, win rate, realized vs. unrealized
* **Distribution** — how the wallet's results spread across its trades

Use it to decide whether a wallet deserves a slot in your 50 — a hot streak on three trades is a different signal than a 60% win rate across three hundred.

***

## Chart Bubbles

Turn on **chart bubbles** for a wallet or group and their buys and sells render directly on any token's chart in Trenches — entry and exit markers over price. It's the fastest way to learn how a profitable wallet actually trades: where they enter, how they scale out, and what they do when a token stalls.
