# Wallets

The **Wallets** page (in the navigation menu) is where your self-custody wallets live.

***

## Your two wallets

Signing in with Google or email gives you two embedded self-custody wallets, created and secured through Privy:

* **Solana wallet** — used by Trenches and Solana swaps
* **EVM wallet** — one address across all supported EVM chains

If you signed in with an existing external wallet instead, that wallet plays the same role. Either way, only you control the keys — onchain.cc can never move your funds.

***

## What you can do here

* **Copy your address** — for deposits from an exchange or another wallet.
* **Send** — transfer any asset to any address.
* **Export your private key** — a guided export puts the key for either embedded wallet in your hands, so your wallets are portable to Phantom, MetaMask, or anywhere else. Do this once and store the keys securely offline.

***

{% hint style="warning" %}
Your private keys and seed phrases are yours alone. No onchain.cc team member will ever ask for them — anyone who does is a scammer. Anyone holding your exported key controls that wallet completely.
{% endhint %}
