# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen — and it's yours to build. Compose it from a library of **30 widgets** on a drag-and-resize grid, keep up to **three saved boards**, and share your layout with anyone.

## Widgets

Widgets cover every corner of the platform. A sample of what's in the library:

* **Portfolio** — Portfolio Overview, Net Worth Chart, Holdings, Wallet Balances, Portfolio PnL, Transaction History, My Trade Log
* **Perps** — Perp Chart & Trade, Open Positions, Open Orders, Closed Positions, Order Book, Market Trades, Funding Rates, Funding Countdown, Funding Payments, Perp Movers, Perp Bubble Map, Account Health
* **Discovery** — Trending, Top Gainers, Watchlist, SOL/ETH Ticker, Meme Position Charts
* **Execution** — **Quick Swap** (the full swap panel as a widget), Alerts Feed
* **Market context** — Crypto News, Economic Calendar, Learn & Guides

Each widget resizes within sensible bounds, and many have their own modes and settings — a trade log filtered to buys, holdings with dust hidden, a chart pinned to your chosen market.

***

## Presets and the setup wizard

Start from a preset and make it yours:

| Preset | Built for |
| --- | --- |
| **General Trader** | Portfolio, market, and trade activity at a glance |
| **True Meme Degen** | Finding the next runner and tracking the players |
| **Pro Perps** | Trading the book with live perps data |
| **Market Watcher** | Trends, movers, and news to read the market |

Or answer the three-question **setup wizard** — what you trade, what belongs at the top, how dense you want it — and it assembles a board for you. Rerun it any time from the Presets menu.

***

## Boards

* Keep up to **three boards**, each with its own name and layout — switch between them with tabs (double-click to rename).
* Add widgets from the library, drag to rearrange, resize freely, and remove with undo.
* Layouts save to your account and follow you across devices.

***

## Sharing boards

Share your board as a card — a schematic of your layout, posted to X or copied as an image, with your handle optionally included. If the recipient opens your link, they get an **"Add this dashboard?"** prompt and can import your exact layout as one of their boards.

***
